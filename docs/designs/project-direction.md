## Vision

### The direction in one paragraph
algo-poc is a solo-operated automated US-equities system whose edge is not strategy cleverness but **evidence discipline**: fail-closed safety defaults, gate epochs, divergence monitoring, and written rules for every capital decision. The project runs two tracks. The **operate track** takes the system from paper to live SGD capital and up a written scaling ladder, one clean evidence epoch at a time. The **research track** develops strategies (sentiment, factors, ML, later regime gating) that earn their way toward capital through a codified promotion pipeline — never by looking good lately. Operate-track incidents preempt research, always.

### 10x Check
The 10x version of this project is not more strategies — it's a system whose every capital decision is pre-written and whose evidence is self-reporting. Twelve months out: live capital sits at whatever rung the ladder's criteria have actually earned; sleeves are promoted and killed by rule; a weekly digest makes the gate decision a five-minute read; and the operator's job is amending rules, not making calls under pressure. (Landscape EUREKA: retail wisdom says evaluate strategies harder; this project's differentiator is the evidence pipeline — strategy work queues behind evidence integrity.)

## Capital-Scaling Ladder (Moderate preset — D4)

Base currency SGD. Epoch = 6 weeks (≈30 trading sessions) at a rung.

| Rung | Capital | Entry requirement |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 5,000 | Go-live gate passed on epoch v2 evidence |
| 1 | 10,000 | One clean epoch at Rung 0 |
| 2 | 20,000 | One clean epoch at Rung 1 |
| 3 | 40,000 | One clean epoch at Rung 2 + explicit capacity review (slippage vs backtest at size) |

**Ladder extension & external constraints (D16):** beyond Rung 3, new rungs exist only by between-epoch amendment — the ladder never scales silently. Every rung remains subject to the IPS's standing constraints: the **30% household satellite ceiling (by dollar)** and **tax/W-8BEN administrative closure before any scale-up**. **Per-rung baselines:** each rung's divergence baseline is regenerated capital-specific (whole-share, commission floor) per the D8 investigation's findings.

**Rung capital defined (ceo-review D16 — ONE quantity governs):** a rung's number is **cumulative SGD funding deployed** to the live account — the only quantity the ladder governs. Retained gains do NOT count against the rung; **losses never change the rung either — only breach criteria act.** Risk %-limits are computed on **current USD NAV** per the IPS (FX translation therefore cannot trip the ladder). De-scaling means reducing deployed exposure to the lower rung's level within 5 sessions; cash may remain in the account.
**Drawdown limit named (D16):** "within IPS limit" = the Gate-3 bound — **max drawdown ≤12% at current size, measured on USD NAV**.
**Action precedence (D16) — one event, one response chain:** safety halt → sleeve demotion → rung de-scale; each level subsumes the ones below it (a halted incident that demotes a sleeve and breaches the epoch is ONE event producing the highest applicable response plus its subsumed records — never three independent punishments).
**Clean epoch =** all of: no live sleeve accumulates an out-of-band divergence run of ≥10 consecutive sessions — a **persisting BREACH state** (rolling windows autocorrelate; this is one event observed for 10 sessions, and that is exactly what the rule measures) · account drawdown within the 12% Gate-3 bound · zero safety incidents (no unhandled halt, no unattributed order, no silent failure discovered) · the per-epoch drill set passed · **minimum evidence quantum (ceo-review D12): ≥15 completed portfolio round-trips AND ≥60% of sessions with live exposure — a shortfall EXTENDS the epoch until met (quiet-and-green is not evidence; numbers amendable between epochs).**
**Definitions (ceo-review D11 — epoch-matched window):** "out-of-band" = divergence monitor status **BREACH** at the pins recorded in the epoch manifest — **window = 30 sessions (matched to epoch length; the 0.30 threshold is re-tuned for the shorter window at rebaseline)**; WARNING does not count. **Verdicts count toward epoch scoring only once the rolling window lies fully inside the current rung** (the clock runs during the grace period; scoring waits) — no rung is graded on the previous rung's data. **Monitor-blind sessions (a missing daily observation on a NYSE trading day — derived, never self-reported) count neither way — the epoch clock pauses; blindness exceeding 5 consecutive sessions is itself a safety incident.**
**Epoch manifest & scoped restart (ceo-review D13):** at epoch start, record the manifest — portfolio weights, sleeve set, baseline id, membership snapshot, divergence window/threshold, cost model, and money-path commit (risk_management / execution / run_paper / shared ledger+liquidation). **Mid-epoch changes to any manifest item or money-path code → the epoch restarts.** Changes outside that scope (docs, CI, research, observability, ML-decision work) never touch the epoch.
**Per-epoch drill set:** one restart/halt drill + one synthetic stop-loss drill per epoch, `__drill__`-tagged, logged with outcomes (the tranche-2 drills are the first instance, then recurring).
**De-scaling rule:** any criterion breached → drop one rung immediately, epoch restarts at the lower rung. **Rung 0 floor:** a breach at Rung 0 disarms the live account entirely (back to paper); re-entry requires a fresh gate review on a new clean epoch. Two consecutive breached epochs at any rung → back to Rung 0 and a full incident review.
**Amendment rule:** ladder edits are allowed only *between* epochs, never during one, and are logged as decisions.

## Strategy Promotion Pipeline (D3.2)

```
RESEARCH ──► SHADOW ──────► PAPER SLEEVE ──────► LIVE SLEEVE
(notebooks,  (shadow scoring (real paper orders,   (real capital within
 backtests)   alongside live  own divergence        the rung's budget)
              pipeline, no    baseline)
              orders)
   gate S:        gate P:            gate L:
   backtest on    ≥8 weeks shadow;   ≥1 full epoch as paper
   point-in-time  IC/hit-rate ≥      sleeve, divergence
   universe w/    pre-registered     in-band; capacity+turnover
   realistic      threshold; no      within limits; kill
   costs (T5      look-ahead audit   criteria defined BEFORE
   standards)     passed             promotion
```

Rules: thresholds are **pre-registered** when the thread starts (no post-hoc goalposts); a sleeve waits out its full window even when early results look great; the sentiment 2026-11 eval is this pipeline's first full new-strategy case. Per-sleeve divergence baselines at the paper gate reuse the T5 rebaseline tooling (membership snapshots, cost model) — budget the operator data-pull cost per promotion, not a fresh archaeology each time. **Gate S and gate P additionally require the edge-validation framework's checks (D10): holdout performance, multiple-testing/deflated-Sharpe correction, parameter stability — matching a backtest proves implementation fidelity, never edge.**

**Incumbent sleeves (ceo-review D9 — grandfathered, not exempt):** the six current sleeves enter live via epoch v2 AS their paper-stage gate, **each with written kill criteria before Rung 0** (the "no written kill criteria → no live promotion" rule is universal), and **each formally evaluated by the edge framework before Rung 0** (D10 — their post-hoc selection after dropping two losers is exactly what the framework corrects for). **Funding rule for promotions:** a newly promoted sleeve takes weight pro-rata from incumbents, capped at 10% of portfolio per promotion; the rebalance is logged.

**What the gate does NOT establish:** divergence-vs-backtest validates implementation fidelity and operational safety. Edge is established only by the edge-validation framework; neither substitutes for the other.

## Per-Sleeve Kill Criteria (D3.3)

A live or paper sleeve is **demoted one stage** when any of:
- Rolling divergence vs its own baseline out-of-band for **10 consecutive sessions**;
- Sleeve drawdown exceeds its per-sleeve budget (set at promotion, default: its backtest max-DD × 1.5);
- Signal staleness (its data source degraded/deprecated — e.g. the sentiment feed's API) for > 5 sessions;
- Any safety incident attributable to the sleeve.
Demoted sleeves may re-earn promotion through the pipeline; retirement (deletion) requires a logged decision. Kill criteria for each sleeve are written **at promotion time** — a sleeve without written kill criteria cannot be promoted to live.

## Evidence State Store (ceo-review 1A)

Epoch/ladder state is DURABLE, in Postgres behind Alembic like every other fact this system trusts — and it stores **only immutable observations, never derived truth (ceo-review D15)**: `divergence_daily` holds exactly the per-sleeve verdict rows the monitor actually produced; `gate_epochs` holds the epoch manifest (D13) and transition EVENTS; drill outcomes are rows. **Blindness is DERIVED — a missing row on a NYSE trading day (via `shared/market_calendar.py`) — so a dead monitor is self-evident rather than expected to report its own death.** Streaks and pause-days are computed at read time by ONE shared query helper used by both the digest and `go_live_gate.py` (the digest's dead-man ping proves delivery; the derived-absence rule proves freshness — different failures, both covered). No epoch fact is ever re-derived from logs or scrollback.

## Operate-Track Observability (D3.4)

Weekly Telegram evidence digest (rides `run_pipeline_report` + the verified bot): equity vs baseline · per-sleeve divergence status · DLQ depth · alerts fired · drills due · epoch progress (week N of 6, criteria green/amber). Plus a 3-line morning digest after the 04:15 run (fills, rejections, halt state). The gate and ladder decisions read this paper trail, not scrollback.

**Digest failure semantics (ceo-review 2A — zero-silent-failures applies to the watcher too):** the digest pings the D13 dead-man check on success — no ping by deadline → external page; a quiet week still sends ("no fills, epoch week N of 6, all green") — the digest NEVER skips; partial sources send with a MISSING-SOURCES banner instead of failing; BLIND/NO_DATA states render as the loudest first line. Digest reads only the evidence state store (1A), never logs.

**Evidence-machinery test set (ceo-review 3A — required by the digest/state-store implementation plan):** (1) golden digest renders per state (all-green / quiet week / MISSING-SOURCES / BLIND-first / breach-streak); (2) the 10-consecutive-session trigger fires at exactly 10 and a 9-OK-9 pattern stays clean; (3) NO_DATA pause-clock arithmetic; (4) rung transitions incl. the Rung-0 disarm floor; (5) drill-outcome recording; (6) dead-man ping asserted on success. Epoch scoring is provably mechanical, not declaredly.

## The accepted PIT coverage bias (D18)

**Decided 2026-08-26.** The point-in-time baseline the ladder depends on cannot be
built from IB Gateway alone. Rather than buy vendor history, the bias is accepted
in writing, bounded by forward capture, and cited wherever it is spent.

This is consistent with the standing direction, not an exception to it: *"Additional
brokers or data vendors — single-broker (IB) simplicity is a feature at this scale"*
(NOT in scope, below).

### The number being accepted

Measured on `output/backtest_multi_20260819_183451.json` (KAN-52, run 2026-08-19):

```
total membership-days    : 1,265,893
excluded membership-days :   142,856   (164 tickers)
excluded_pct             :     11.28%
floor_pct                :      5.00%
state                    : BLOCKED
```

Of the 11.28%, **10.38%** is genuine index departures (146 tickers) and **0.91%** is
resolution failures on names still in the index (18 tickers). Recovering every one of
the latter still leaves 10.38%, so the gap is not repairable in code. IB serves no
bars for departed names by symbol *or* by `conId` — probed 2026-08-20: four of five
resolve a `conId` and return zero bars, every match a `primaryExchange=VALUE` stub.

Shortening the window does not rescue it either. Only a 1-year window clears the
floor (4.26%); 2y is 5.04% and 3y is 5.69%. A 1-year backtest cannot carry the DSR
and holdout evidence, which is already marginal at DSR 0.868 against a 0.95 threshold.

### What it means for anything resting on this baseline

The baseline prices only names IB still serves, so it is **survivorship-biased
upward by an unmeasured amount**. Index departures skew toward underperformers;
excluding roughly a tenth of membership-days therefore flatters backtest returns
rather than merely adding noise. Every conclusion drawn from it — Rung-0 economics,
the D10 verdicts, any divergence comparison — inherits that optimism.

"Unmeasured" is exact and not hedging: the size of the bias cannot be estimated from
this data, because estimating it would require the very history that is missing.

### The floor is not moved

`backtest/membership.py`'s `DEFAULT_COVERAGE_FLOOR_PCT` stays at **5.0**, and
affected artifacts continue to report `state: BLOCKED` with `is_like_for_like`
False. The bias is accepted in this record, **not** by relaxing the gate.

That distinction is the whole point. `is_like_for_like` gates both the divergence
monitor and `run_sleeve_evaluation.py`, so moving the floor would silently change
what every future run accepts as evidence — the exact failure the "never silently
degraded" rule exists to prevent. A reader who opens a `BLOCKED` artifact should
see that it is blocked and come here to find out why it was spent anyway.

### How the decision is enforced (KAN-68)

D18 shipped as prose and doc-guards, with no representation in the type that
gates on it. That left the D10 verdicts (KAN-55) unable to run at all: their
precondition wanted `coverage.state == OK`, which this decision made
permanently unreachable, and the only override —
`--allow-non-comparable-baseline` — stamps the run gate-invalid and refuses to
spend the holdout of record. Both doors were shut.

`research/bias_acceptances.json` is what makes the decision executable.
`backtest/bias_acceptance.py` resolves a baseline to one of three states rather
than a bool:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `VALID` | Nothing needed excusing |
| `VALID_WITH_ACCEPTED_BIAS` | Coverage is `BLOCKED`, and this decision accepts it **for this exact artifact** |
| `INVALID` | Everything else |

The value is in how little the middle state excuses. An acceptance applies only
when the artifact is like-for-like in every *other* respect, so a same-bar
baseline, a missing commission floor, or a static present-day universe is
refused with a valid acceptance in hand — those are different defects that
nobody decided about. Acceptance is pinned to one `source_sha256` and
cross-checked against the artifact's own coverage figures, so it cannot bless
the next re-run, and it lapses automatically if the measured bias drifts away
from the 11.28% recorded here. `MISSING` coverage can never be accepted: you
can only accept a bias you measured.

`ExecutionModel.is_like_for_like` is deliberately untouched by all of this, and
the divergence monitor keeps gating on it — so this decision cannot un-blind
daily drift reporting as a side effect. That remains a separate, open question.

Acceptance is a commit, not a keystroke: like `research/holdout_registry.json`,
the registry's git history — not its caller-supplied `accepted_at` field — is
the evidence that a bias was accepted *before* it was spent.

### Re-evidence trigger

**When forward capture has accumulated 3 years of continuous daily bars for the
whole index, re-run the baseline over that window and re-evidence.** Forward
capture ends this problem structurally: bars are captured while a name is still a
member, so its history is already held when it later departs, and IB's refusal to
serve delisted history stops mattering for any window beginning after capture starts.

Three years is the first point at which a window is both long enough to be worth
re-running and clears the floor honestly — the 3-year window sits at 5.69% today,
and forward capture drives that toward ~0%.

**Capture start date: 2026-08-18.** That is the earliest session date now held in
`ohlcv_daily`, and it is the start of the 3-year re-evidence clock: **a baseline
re-run under this trigger takes a window beginning 2026-08-18, and the trigger
matures no earlier than 2029-08-18.** KAN-58 built the writer, PR #91 connected
`data_ingestion` to IB, and the change has now reached a running container —
so the bias is time-bounded rather than open-ended, which is the distinction the
previous wording of this paragraph turned on.

Verified 2026-08-30: 4,336 rows, 542 tickers, 8 session dates 2026-08-18 through
2026-08-27, no gaps across US trading days in that span.

**Session date is not ingest date, and the gap between them is the point.** The
first *ingest* ran 2026-08-27 16:49 UTC and the second 2026-08-28 16:57 UTC;
between them they pulled the eight sessions above. Capture is prospective in the
sense that matters here — every one of the 542 names was a live member when its
bars were pulled, so none of this history depends on IB serving a delisted name
later. The clock therefore starts at the earliest *session* held, not at the
first ingest run.

**Continuity is not yet established, and the trigger requires it.** The trigger
above asks for 3 years of *continuous* daily bars. As of 2026-08-30 the
2026-08-28 session (a Friday) is not present, and only two ingest runs have
occurred in the twelve days since the first session. Eight sessions is far too
short to demonstrate a daily cadence either way. **Until capture is observed
running daily without gaps for a sustained period, treat the clock as started
but the continuity requirement as unmet** — a run of bars with holes in it does
not satisfy this trigger, and discovering that in 2029 would be the expensive
way to find out. Any permanent capture gap must be recorded — the paper-run
equivalent is `shared/absent_sessions.py`, the registry KAN-67 added for the
2026-08-13 and 2026-08-18 absences; capture has no such registry yet.

## Decisions made

- Vibe-Trading was treated as a research reference, not a replacement trading
  system. Existing trading surfaces remain authoritative.
- Alpha-Zoo-like research belongs in a native research layer with point-in-time
  factors, shadow candidates, validation gates, and explicit promotion into a
  small bounded live-research sleeve.
- Research may affect an already-held stock's score, but promotion/execution
  remains governed by portfolio, risk, liquidity, duplication, and exposure
  constraints rather than raw research rank alone.
- Paper deployable capital must come from the broker's NetLiquidation and a
  configurable deployment fraction/cap, not the historical local seed value.
- PostgreSQL is the durable source of truth for capital snapshots, positions,
  order intents, executions, reservations, and lifecycle state.
- Signals are proposals, not fills. Only idempotently projected IB executions
  may mutate cash and positions.
- Reconciliation is fail-closed. The system does not silently repair or guess
  broker account, contract, or sleeve attribution.
- Entry rollout remains disabled by default. Risk-reducing exits remain
  available only after broker and sleeve-owned quantity validation.
- Backups remain local under `~/ibc/backups`; no rsync or other copy to iCloud.

## Paper-run and divergence investigation

The paper run initially failed because IB Gateway was not authenticated. After
manual login, the run was retried. Allocation rejections then exposed the
larger issue: paper sizing relied on local sleeve seed capital rather than the
approximately $1 million broker paper NAV, and proposed orders could burst
exposure because gates did not reserve same-cycle notional.

The 2026-07-22 Telegram message reported:

```text
paper run OK — B:1 S:0 skip:55 | divergence: BREACH | 1 resting orders |
today's snapshot ✓
```

The thematic-momentum breach was investigated against
`output/backtest_multi_20260721_053247.json` and the divergence log. There were
only five overlapping days, but the discrepancy was real:

- paper thematic return: approximately `-5.9%`;
- backtest thematic return: approximately `-0.3%`;
- absolute gap: approximately `-5.62 percentage points`;
- correlation: approximately `-0.459`;
- paper held nine thematic positions despite `top_n=8`;
- expected CSCO, AMD, AMAT, and ARKG exits did not occur;
- thematic paper logs contained no sells;
- sleeve gross exposure reached approximately `137%`, above the intended
  `120%` ceiling.

Root causes:

1. Strategy factories recreated fresh closure state every paper day. Durable
   positions were visible to the risk gate but not to strategy exit logic, so
   restart-held lots could not trigger trailing, time, or rotation exits.
2. Exposure gates checked current exposure without including the proposed
   order/reservations, allowing a new buy to overshoot the ceiling.
3. A blank-slate paper start naturally differs from a continuous backtest that
   already owns positions, but this did not explain the missing exits or
   exposure overshoot.

Tasks 4, 8, and 9 address these defects. A short cold-start divergence window
is still expected, but it is no longer acceptable to dismiss missing exits as
startup noise.

### Task 6 — idempotent fill projection

Implemented the fill projector so only immutable IB executions update sleeve
cash, positions, trades, and order filled quantity. Replayed executions are
idempotent; rejected executions remain durable audit records.

Independent review exposed that rejected audit rows could later be inferred as
applied. Corrections added an atomic `projection_applied` outcome, applied-only
reconstruction, UTC timestamp normalization, and real migration tests.

Migration safety:

- the projection-outcome migration requires an empty legacy
  `execution_fills` table because old rows cannot be safely classified as
  applied or rejected;
- it fails before mutation and requires manual reconciliation if rows exist;
- SQLite online upgrade/downgrade is tested;
- offline SQL generation is explicitly refused because the safety precondition
  requires an online query.

### Task 8 — strategy-context hydration

Added immutable `PortfolioContext`, `HeldPosition`, and `PendingOrder` state.
All six active strategy factories now support context-backed paper evaluation:

- momentum;
- sector rotation;
- quality value;
- earnings drift;
- thematic momentum;
- tail-risk hedge.

Context mode is restart-safe and does not mutate closure state. Context-free
mode preserves existing backtest simulation. Hydration includes quantity,
average entry, durable peak, entry date, account-scoped active intents,
remaining pending quantity, sleeve budget, and reservations.

Safety behavior added during review:

- pending buys suppress duplicate buys;
- opposing active buys suppress crossed exits;
- pending sells reduce, rather than completely suppress, uncovered exit
  quantity;
- multiple same-symbol pending orders are preserved and summed;
- `APPROVED`, `SUBMITTED`, and `PARTIALLY_FILLED` intents hydrate consistently;
- quality-value trailing exits work even when fresh fundamentals are missing;
- paper signal generation never records a speculative fill.

### Task 7 — fail-closed broker reconciliation

Implemented immutable broker account snapshots, an async IB account reader,
contract-keyed reconciliation, report-only CLI output, reviewed repair plans,
and a guarded paper-only apply path.

Safety properties:

- exactly one IB managed account is required;
- paper/live account prefixes are validated;
- positions reconcile by account and contract, orders by account and IB order
  ID, with absolute quantity tolerance `1e-6`;
- only applied execution audits contribute to filled totals; unapplied audits
  remain blocking discrepancies;
- any mismatch blocks entries and is never silently auto-corrected;
- IB-only positions require explicit sleeve attribution;
- repair requires a real TTY, exact confirmation, a pre-mutation paper-state
  dump, a `DU` account, no unresolved mappings, exact validated action schema,
  and one transaction;
- malformed, non-finite, missing, unreadable, ambiguous, or cross-account plans
  fail closed before backup or mutation;
- `positions.account_id` is additive and nullable for legacy safety; legacy
  NULL ownership is never guessed and blocks entries;
- projection and repair lock by `con_id` across sleeves/ticker aliases before
  selecting the exact account+sleeve+contract target.

### Task 9 — reconciled daily orchestration

The daily paper sequence is now:

```text
IB snapshot
  -> fail-closed reconciliation
  -> deployable capital and CapitalSnapshot
  -> account-scoped hydrated strategy contexts and reservations
  -> signals with same-cycle projected exposure
  -> deterministic PROPOSED intents
  -> broker-revalidated durable outbox publication
  -> risk lifecycle persistence
  -> execution
  -> idempotent fill projection
```

Delivered behavior:

- `--entries-disabled` defaults on; buys also require the selected capital-mode
  configuration to enable entries;
- reconciliation/rollout failures suppress buys but preserve validated exits;
- deterministic IDs include date, account, mode, sleeve, symbol, and side;
- current-cycle accepted buy notional is reserved before evaluating subsequent
  candidates, preventing allocation bursts;
- unpublished intents replay idempotently with the same ID;
- old unpublished sells are revalidated against current broker holdings and
  active broker/durable sells before publication;
- oversized stale sells are cancelled and atomically superseded by a
  deterministic capped intent; zero headroom suppresses publication;
- ambiguous Redis acknowledgement replays the same safe replacement;
- risk requires a matching durable intent and treats its symbol, side,
  quantity, price, portfolio, account, and mode as authoritative;
- missing or corrupted intents fail closed;
- risk refreshes account/mode positions and the matching capital snapshot for
  every recommendation instead of relying on constructor-time state;
- stale capital blocks buys, while exits use account/sleeve-owned quantity and
  remain retryable if validation state is temporarily unavailable;
- one sleeve cannot liquidate another sleeve's holding in the same ticker;
- insufficient durable sleeve attribution becomes `RISK_REJECTED` without
  publishing an approved order;
- lifecycle transitions are persisted before Redis publication;
- restart-safe lifecycle gauges are labeled by account, mode, and status;
- quality-value sizing changed from `10%` to `6%` and thematic momentum from
  `15%` to `13.5%` in paper and backtest builders.
